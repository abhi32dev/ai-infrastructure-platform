Critical invariants

  • Voice transport is never authoritative for interview progress.
  • The standard provider key never enters browser code.
  • Private reference answers are not returned by question-list APIs.
  • Invalid answers retry the same turn rather than silently advancing.
  • Realtime failure degrades to local voice or text without losing state.
  • Terminal completion persists a scored readiness artifact.
